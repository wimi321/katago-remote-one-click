# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a welcoming place for people of every background and experience level.

Be respectful, patient, and constructive. Focus criticism on ideas and code, assume good intent, and avoid harassment, insults, discrimination, threats, or publishing another person's private information. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential to the extent practical.
